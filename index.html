⚔️ Simple 2D RPG ⚔️

Defeat all enemies to win the game!

Controls

  • Arrow Keys or WASD - Move
  • Space Bar - Attack

Stats

Health: 100/100

Level: 1

Experience: 0

Enemies: 3

Tips

  • 🔵 You are the blue square
  • 🔴 Red squares are enemies
  • ⬜ Gray squares are obstacles
  • Get close to enemies and press SPACE to attack
  • Level up by defeating enemies!
